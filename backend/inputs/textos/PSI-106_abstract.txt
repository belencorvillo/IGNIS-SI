HYPOTHESIS:
The hypothesis is that the structure and stability of laminar diffusion flames in microgravity are significantly influenced by burner diameter, coflow velocity, and fuel composition and are fundamentally different than flames observed on Earth.

RESEARCH IMPACTS / EARTH BENEFITS:
1. Improved numerical models for flame behavior lead to more efficient combustion technologies on Earth. 2. Understanding soot formation aids in designing combustion systems with minimum emissions. 3. Insights into flame stability and behavior to enhance fire safety. 4. Findings will inform the development of cleaner, more efficient engines and industrial burners.
